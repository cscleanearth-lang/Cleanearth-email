Hello,

I am Eyram from Cleanearth. For almost 30 years, we have helped industrial companies across Africa get the water they need. We design and build treatment plants, and we run them every day.

Water is getting harder for industry to manage. Rules on wastewater are stricter, water prices are rising, and older plants cost more to run each year.
HERE IS HOW WE HELP
01  Design and build (EPC)
We build plants that treat your water and wastewater. This includes membrane filters that remove impurities (RO, reverse osmosis, and UF, ultrafiltration), bacteria-based treatment of wastewater (MBR, membrane bioreactor, and SBR, sequencing batch reactor), and systems that recycle all the water so none is released (ZLD, zero liquid discharge).
02  Running your plant (O&M)
We operate your plant day and night and keep it in good condition. You can also choose BOO (Build-Own-Operate) or BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer). We pay to build the plant and run it, and you pay only for the water treated, so you spend nothing upfront. With BOT, we hand the plant over to you after an agreed period.
03  Chemicals, spare parts and support
We supply the chemicals used to treat water and the spare parts. When something goes wrong, we send quick technical help.
04  Testing and better performance
We test your water in the lab and track its quality live. We also use helpful bacteria to break down waste (bioremediation), so your existing plants work better.
